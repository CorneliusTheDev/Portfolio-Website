Why play SYNC

Built for chaos
designed for fun

01 🔗
Stay in Sync

Every player controls one part of the machine. Nobody wins alone. Coordinate or collapse — the choice is yours.

02 🌀
Dynamic Levels

Environments shift and evolve mid-run. No two sessions play the same. Always fresh, always unpredictable.

03 🎤
Voice Puzzles

Some doors only open when your whole team shouts together. Literally. Use your mic or panic in silence.

04 ⚡
Power-Up Combos

Stack abilities with your teammates for wild effect combinations. Some combos are OP. We left them in on purpose.

05 🏆
Ranked Runs

Compete on global leaderboards. Time your runs, rate your team's sync score, and climb the ladder together.

06 🛋️
Couch & Online

Split-screen local coop or full online matchmaking. Works with any controller. Works with keyboards too, we guess.
